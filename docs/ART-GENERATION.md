# Bushido Ops artwork generation record

Mode: built-in image generation and reference-image editing. Three transparent eight-frame sprite sheets and one clean-background edit were generated. The app renders the source sheets on a 64 × 56 grid with sixteen colors to meet the subsequently approved 8-bit style. Only the background crops listed in CHARACTERS.md are used; other regions retain the original reference.
